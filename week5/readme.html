Traces of a Day

Traces of a Day is an abstract p5.js clock that explores time through visual layers rather than traditional numbers. Inspired by the soft, translucent texture of traditional Hanji paper, the project represents the quiet accumulation of time as semi-transparent color blocks gently stack on top of one another at the center of the canvas. As hours pass, new layers build up, creating a rich visual record of the day.

In the Morning around (9:00 AM) it starts on a light, clean background with just a few soft pastel shapes in pale blue and yellow. The composition feels calm, open, and still. And in the afternoon (3:00 PM) the warm orange, subtle purples, and muted greens begin to overlay the morning foundation. As the layers overlap, the piece gains density and texture, reflecting the active pace of the day. And finally at night (9:00 PM) the canvas darkens into deep indigo and charcoal tones. The earlier colors submerge beneath darker layers, forming a rich, tactile depth that captures the quiet weight of memory and time.
